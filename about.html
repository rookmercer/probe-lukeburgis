About

He has lived the question his work tries to answer.

Luke Burgis is the author of Wanting, a professor at The Catholic University of America, and the founder of the Cluny Institute. He studies the hidden forces that shape human desire — imitation, rivalry, contagion — and what they do to individuals, institutions, and cultures.

Four lives, one question.
01

Wall Street

NYU Stern School of Business. Two years in finance. He left.

The desires weren't his own.

The Work

Each departure taught the same lesson: the desires he was chasing weren't his own. They were borrowed. That lesson became his life's work.

The question found him.
