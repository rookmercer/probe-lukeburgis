About

He has lived the question his work tries to answer.

Luke Burgis is the author of Wanting, a professor at The Catholic University of America, and the founder of the Cluny Institute. He studies the hidden forces that shape human desire — imitation, rivalry, contagion — and what they do to individuals, institutions, and cultures.

Four lives, one question.

The work.

Wanting: The Power of Mimetic Desire in Everyday Life

St. Martin's Press · 2021

Wanting made the ideas of the social theorist René Girard legible to a general audience. It has been translated into more than twenty languages. Tyler Cowen called it “the go-to book” on mimetic desire.

The One and the Ninety-Nine

St. Martin's Press · 2026

Takes the theory into the age of social contagion: how crowds form, how identities harden, and how the individual survives the group.

He is also the co-author, with Joshua Miller, of Unrepeatable: Cultivating the Unique Calling of Every Person, and the editor of Be Not Conformed: René Girard at the Nexus of Athens, Jerusalem, and Silicon Valley.

More than 37,000 readers follow The Luke Burgis Newsletter — ideas on desire, identity, culture, and work, delivered when they're ready, not on a schedule.
