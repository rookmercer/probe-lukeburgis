Luke Burgis

Author  ·  Professor  ·  Founder

Hero portrait photo — B&W or muted color [NEEDS LUKE]

Luke Burgis is the author of Wanting: The Power of Mimetic Desire in Everyday Life and The One and the Ninety-Nine. He is a professor at The Catholic University of America.

His work applies the insights of René Girard — particularly the theory of mimetic desire — to questions of leadership, culture, and the way human beings form their identities. He has spoken at organizations and universities around the world.

He was interviewed by Tyler Cowen in April 2026. Cowen called him "one of my favorite current thinkers" (Conversations with Tyler, Ep. 287, April 30, 2026).
