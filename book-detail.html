About the Book

Why do we want what other people want? In Wanting, Luke Burgis draws on the work of French philosopher René Girard to show that desire is mimetic — we borrow our wants from models around us. The implications ripple through every part of life: careers, relationships, markets, and culture. Wanting is the essential guide to understanding the hidden engine of human behavior.

On the illustrations

A collaboration with Liana Finck

"I had realized that my nonfiction book needed simple illustrations to reinforce its ideas — I'm such a visual thinker that I knew I would benefit from someone who could help me 'see' the ideas in new ways." The drawings needed to be no-frills, witty, biting, and thought-provoking to balance out the sometimes heavily philosophical nature of the text. "When I first laid eyes on Liana's drawings, I felt deeply understood — which is odd, considering that I'd never even met Liana or seen her work before."
