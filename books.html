Works by Luke Burgis

Wanting

St. Martin's Press · 2021

Why do we want what other people want? Drawing on the work of René Girard, Luke Burgis shows that desire is mimetic — we borrow our wants from models around us. The implications ripple through every part of life: careers, relationships, markets, and culture.

The One and the Ninety-Nine

St. Martin's Press · 2026

How cultures protect — or destroy — the individual. A follow-up to Wanting that applies mimetic theory to the tension between individual identity and group conformity.
