Unrepeatable

Cultivating the Unique Calling of Every Person

Every person has a calling that is theirs alone. Unrepeatable is about that conviction and its consequence: that the people around us — parents, teachers, mentors, managers — have a responsibility to help draw it out.

Written with Joshua Miller, it is a book about vocation as something cultivated rather than found, for anyone responsible for another person’s growth.
