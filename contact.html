A note from Luke

A few things I can’t do:

I say this with genuine regret, because the volume of thoughtful mail I receive is one of the better parts of this work.

  • •I don’t read unsolicited manuscripts, book proposals, or business plans, and my team will return them unopened. This protects both of us.
  • •I’m not able to offer personal advice, counsel, or feedback on individual situations, however carefully described. What I have to say on those questions is in the books, the essays, and the newsletter.
  • •I don’t take exploratory calls, “quick coffees,” or advisory conversations outside of formal engagements.
  • •Direct messages on social platforms aren’t monitored. This page is the only door.

— Luke
