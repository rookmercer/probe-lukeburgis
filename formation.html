Something is wrong at work.

Burnout. Quiet quitting. The nagging sense that your ambitions are borrowed — that you're chasing goals that were never really yours. These aren't management failures. They're desire failures.

Most of us have never been taught to want well. We absorb goals from our peers, our culture, our algorithms — and then wonder why reaching them doesn't feel like we thought it would.

Work as Soulcraft is a program about learning to want differently. It won't make you more productive. It will help you figure out what's actually worth doing.
