Questions worth returning to

Why do we want what other people want?

Because desire is mimetic — we borrow our wants from models, and those borrowed wants move everything from markets to marriages.

What happens to individuals inside institutions?

Conformity is quiet and contagious — the question is whether cultures protect the individual or slowly dissolve them into the group.

What makes work worth doing?

Burnout is a desire problem before it's a management problem — work hollows out when it stops being a place where a person can become fully themselves.
