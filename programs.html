Online Course

Work as Soulcraft

forming a person in the age of intelligent machines

An 8-module program on discernment, agency, and building a life that is actually yours. Rooted in the ideas of Wanting and The One and the Ninety-Nine.

For founders, executives, and professionals who want work to be a place of formation — not just production.

  • Diagnose the desires driving your decisions.
  • Bring Discernment to your next move.
  • Rebuild your relationship with work from the inside out.

02

Half-Day Workshop

Foundations of Agency

A half-day workshop for corporate teams on mimetic desire, identity, and what drives human behavior at work. Grounded in the ideas of Wanting.

Delivered by Luke in person. The same diagnostic frameworks that inform his keynotes, applied to your organization for a morning.

For leadership teams, executive offsites, and organizations seeking a shared diagnostic language.

  • Name the mimetic dynamics driving team behavior.
  • Diagnose rivalry and incentive traps before they escalate.
  • Leave with a shared language for desire, identity, and agency.
