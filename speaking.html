Master the hidden forces quietly directing energy away from what matters.

Luke Burgis speaks to boards, executive teams, and conference audiences about the hidden dynamics that shape what organizations actually do.

Keynote Talks

Each keynote is up to 1 hour long, including Q&A. Format is flexible, including fireside chat. Luke does not do panels.

1

Moving from Thin Desires to Thick Desires

Learn the hidden forces of desire operating in the organization and in your life. Attendees will leave with a checklist and inventory of relational hazards and opportunities to diagnose and improve upon immediately, along with a framework for operating better in teams. The skills they learn they will be able to take with them into their families and friendships.

2

Strengthening Teams Through Differentiation

Concrete guidance for individuals within teams to differentiate themselves, reject passive conformity, and beginning moving at the speed of the truth. Every team member will leave with an understanding of what healthy communication and communion looks and feels like within a team.

3

A More Agentic Culture

Luke will explain what real agency means and give everyone a framework for thinking through agency in the context of relationships, with a roadmap to help each team member take concrete actions to contribute without being asked or told, and to seize opportunities to add value as a core part of their responsibility. Everyone leaves inspired to act with more freedom to live out the mission of the organization.

4

The Four Courages

Luke inspires everyone to live out four forms of courage in the workplace: the courage to “be” and show up daily, the courage to be oneself, the courage to be a part, and the courage to be in communion. The four courages, taken together, are the foundation of a thriving company culture.

Executive Workshops & Retreats
