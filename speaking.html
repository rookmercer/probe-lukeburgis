Topics

Five keynotes, each grounded in Luke's research on mimetic desire, identity, and culture. Customized for your audience.

Wanting: The Hidden Driver of Every Decision Your Team Makes

Mimetic desire applied to leadership: why smart teams chase the wrong goals, how incentives really work, and how to build a culture that wants the right things.

Best for: executive offsites, leadership summits

The One and the Ninety-Nine: How Great Cultures Protect the Individual

Conformity is quiet and contagious. A framework for building organizations where people don't dissolve into the herd — creativity and belonging without groupthink.

Best for: all-hands, culture & HR leaders

Work as Soulcraft: Rebuilding Meaning at Work

Burnout, disengagement, and quiet quitting are desire problems before they're management problems. A serious account of what makes work worth doing.

Best for: future-of-work conferences, HR leadership

Beyond Rivalry: What Leaders Get Wrong About Competition

Mimetic rivalry drives markets — and poisons companies from the inside. How to compete without becoming what you're fighting.

Best for: sales kickoffs, executive teams, strategy forums

Attention, Desire, and the Human Person in the Age of AI

What remains irreducibly human as machines get better at mimicking us — and what that means for how we build companies.

Best for: tech companies, universities, idea festivals
