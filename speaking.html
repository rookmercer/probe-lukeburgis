Offerings

Keynote

Mimetic Desire and the Organization

A diagnosis of the imitation patterns that silently drive hiring, strategy, and culture — drawn from Wanting and applied to the questions your audience is actually facing. Built for conferences, board meetings, and annual gatherings. No two keynotes are the same talk.

Half-Day Workshop

Applied Mimetic Analysis

A working session for leadership teams: the frameworks applied directly to the rivalries, incentives, and imitation patterns inside your organization. Pre-work for participants; a shared diagnostic language by the end. The room leaves with a map of the dynamics no one was naming.

Full Executive Retreat

Diagnosis Through Application

The complete arc for organizations at an inflection point — succession, acquisition, rapid growth, cultural drift. Each participant works through the mimetic forces shaping their own decisions. The team leaves with a shared framework and individual action plans.
